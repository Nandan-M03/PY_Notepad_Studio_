Get started

On Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Safari)

  1. Click Install App in the header or browser address bar.
  2. PY Notepad runs as a fast standalone window.
  3. Works offline on any device.

On Phone or Tablet (Android, iOS)

  1. Tap Open in browser.
  2. Tap Install App, or on iOS choose Share ⎋ → Add to Home Screen ⊞.
  3. Launches full-screen with offline storage.

Files are saved in the browser of the device you use, so use Export ZIP inside the app to back up or move projects.

Good to know

PY Notepad runs Python inside your browser via WebAssembly. Files remain private on your device. Packages with pure Python or WebAssembly builds run locally.
